Use case: stylized-concept.
Create an original premium hand-painted underwater environment backdrop for Tidekin, a warm ocean exploration game. Wide landscape 1536 x 1024 (3:2), full opaque image, entire frame submerged underwater. NO air, no sky, no waterline, no surface above frame. NO characters, creatures, text, UI, icons, rubbish, trash, boats or buildings.
Art direction: richly illustrated storybook adventure environment with convincing dimensional geology and beautifully brushed material detail, luminous turquoise caustics, deep teal foreground shadows, jewel-toned coral and warm limestone. High visual richness, not flat procedural cyan and not a photorealistic photograph.
Composition for a side-view swimming game background: layered eroded limestone towers and underwater rock arches frame the left and right sides. Foreground side formations crop naturally at the image edges; weathered cream and honey limestone has cavities, scalloped ledges, algae and delicate rippling blue caustic patterns. Large coral fans, soft branching coral, sponges and gracefully curved kelp cluster along lower corners and side ledges, using salmon, peach, ochre and sea-green accents. Asymmetric interesting silhouettes and a few floating underwater rock forms/overhangs in the mid-distance add depth. Seabed descends toward the center. The middle 45 percent of the composition remains an unobstructed readable SWIMMING CORRIDOR with translucent cyan water, soft distant rock spires and subtle bands of filtered light: not featureless empty fill, but lower contrast and detail than framing edges. Central water is medium luminous turquoise so foreground gameplay can read against it. At the bottom middle include softly receding reef shelves well behind traversal, with faint caustics; keep strong foreground objects on the sides. Distant limestone pillars recede through several layers of misty underwater cyan. Shafts of submerged sunlight filter diagonally from outside the upper frame, gentle suspended particles, tangible depth.
Detail density: rich and beautiful across the whole environment, natural forms at varied scales, painterly depth and atmospheric perspective. Coral fans and kelp should feel lush but never clog the middle traversal route. No giant flat blue empty rectangle. No black void, no neon glow, no hard vignette, no split-level scene, no surface-air strip, no text or UI. This must look like finished game environment art, not a concept sheet or UI mockup. Opaque edge-to-edge painted pixels.
