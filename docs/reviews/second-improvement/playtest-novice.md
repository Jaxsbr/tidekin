## Actionable observations, distinct from flags

- **Opening purpose is indirect (old build).** The raft asks the player to sail; cleanup purpose arrives in Cabin. This agent explored supplies before diving. Consider a small first-session invitation to visit the reef, or make the cabin prompt more discoverable. This is a clarity observation, not a claim that players cannot discover Space.
- **Steering/bearings need a learning step (old build).** East salvage plus W did not tell this novice what direction the raft was facing. Wrong initial route was caught by the chart. A legible heading cue near helm instructions could reduce chart reopening. No claim of incorrect steering physics.
- **Chart leaves helm (old build).** Map checks reset sailing to “Click the wheel to sail.” H was needed again. More explicit transition feedback would help; movement did not silently continue.
- **Surface return wording deserves reproduction (old build).** “Swim up to return” remained after restoration; 2.6s W did not leave the view, whereas Space did. This may be incomplete ascent or restored-mode input behavior; no source investigation performed. Prefer wording that explicitly identifies the demonstrated Space exit unless ascent is verified to work.
- **Pickup feedback is strong.** Nearby targets gain specific Collect labels, pickup counters advance, and gathering names quantity. Wrong-distance messages suggest a correction. Successful restoration feels complete through removed trash, visible restored text and toast, rather than merely a hidden QA flag.
- **Failure/retry is strong.** Reset consequences are stated and demonstrably applied. Surface refill retained partial cleanup. Agent pauses mean this session cannot establish whether the air budget is appropriate for humans.
- **Workshop loop works.** Held/cost counts and missing-resource calls to action provided a clear route to a useful first build. Unlock toast and rig choices made expenditure consequential. After finding scrap, the map gave exact range and target; no unexplained resource granting occurred.
- **Responsive evidence is limited.** Wide opening and square restored/cabin screens were visually inspected. Restored square HUD fit and remained legible. This is not a full narrow-screen audit; no square active-air gameplay test was performed.
- **Build transition recovery is separate.** Failed scene import occurred across a deployment change. Recovery UI protected the save and Reload worked. Do not present this as a stable final-build WebGL failure.
