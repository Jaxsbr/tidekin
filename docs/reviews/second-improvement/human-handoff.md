# Jaco’s next hands-on review

Open the existing local preview at http://127.0.0.1:5181 in the same browser/profile as your voyage. Continue keeps your prior supplies and raft progress. Start new voyage replaces that profile’s save only after confirmation; use another browser profile if you want a fresh opening without replacing it.

Try the opening without reading the full controls first. Explore the raft objects, take and leave the helm, inspect the cabin/workbench, then try the nearby reef cleanup. Notice whether the next action and its purpose are clear from the game.

For the cleanup check: collect some trash, surface to breathe, descend again, then deliberately run out of air. Retry directly and see whether everything from that attempt resets. Complete the reef, return, open the map and reload. Previously earned raft progress and the restored site should remain.

Useful feedback:

1. What did you want to do first, and what did you try that did not respond as expected?
2. Did steering and changing views feel predictable? Could you see the character, targets and bag clearly?
3. Did gathering/building and cleaning the reef feel worthwhile? What changed that you noticed?

The agent sessions can verify behavior and expose friction, but they cannot establish your enjoyment or a reason to return. This is one small cleanup site and the existing short upgrade arc; it is not a claim of a full game or long-term retention.

Full controls remain in Pause and the README. Unfinished cleanup resets on browser reload; live pause retains it. Restoration commits on the final trash pickup, with no extra escape challenge. Watch for a persistent save-error banner before testing reload.
