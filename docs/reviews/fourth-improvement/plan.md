## Gates established before integrated review

1. All interfaces: original illustration dominates meaningful visual areas; concise labels retain necessary action clarity. Title, HUD, workshop, chart, journal, cabin, inspection, pause, settings and dive reviewed at 1440×900 and 900×900. No overflow or hidden primary action. Text reductions alone do not satisfy.
2. Workshop: one eligible current plan, materials pictured with counts, Build; no future/locked plans, unlock prose or speed percentages. Actual attachment animation visibly changes the raft with controlled camera and completion feedback, reduced-motion alternative.
3. Controls: immediate third-person raft steering initially, after modal close, reload and dive return. No helm mechanism, first person, practice or comparison UI/runtime modes. Directional swimming and pointer pulling fixed at 2.2.
4. Character/world: richer silhouette, face/hair/clothing and articulated strokes. Prone side-profile propulsion, eased upright idle, horizontal surface crawl. No trailing basket. Camera scrolls left/right/down at readable character scale and pointer hit testing remains aligned.
5. Collectibles: only the current near/overlapping object gets one compact square illustrated card. One or two words; no remote names, distances, arrows, poles or sail-closer instructions. Animated appearance/removal.
6. Functional: real pointer/keyboard journey, earned resources and build without fixture claims; persistence and failure/reset tests. Fixtures clearly marked for visual coverage beyond the earned route.
7. Performance: same machine, Chrome, 1440×900, DPR1, High, 5-second warmup and 20-second sample, exclusive browser measurement; report observed values and limitations.

Independent review must enumerate defects per gate rather than hiding failure in a composite score. Iterate on material visual/functional defects found. Sound remains unassessed unless audibly reviewed.
